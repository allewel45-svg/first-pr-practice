How the layout works

Photos show an L-ish patio: long covered dining along the stone veneer wall, a TV/white-wall corner, kitchen pass-through, and a larger open courtyard with the central tree. Existing bulky sofas are cleared from the main floor; one VIP lounge strip remains for the birthday guest and elders.

  • Zone A — three 8′ banquet tables under the roof for main seated meal (30)
  • Zone B — organic balloon arch + wildflower “Happy Birthday” backdrop + cake
  • Zone C — one-way buffet fed from the kitchen opening; drinks beside the fridge
  • Zone D — four rounds around the tree + six high-tops for cocktail overflow
  • Zone E — utility/restroom corridor stays clear (no guest seating)

Aisles: 36″ minimum at every door; 48″ main spine from courtyard → dining → buffet. Support posts stay as fixed obstacles; tables offset around them.
